# OUS Interview Prep — CLAUDE.md

## Purpose
Help Ingebjørg prepare for her PhD stipendiat interview at Oslo University Hospital (OUS),
Seksjon for klinisk psykoseforskning. Project: "The night and day of bipolar disorder."

## How to run a session
1. Start every session with: /prime
2. Choose a mode: /quiz, /deep-dive, or /panel
3. Save useful answers to outputs/ for review

## Available commands
- /prime       — Load all context about the position, project, and research group
- /quiz        — Mock interview Q&A with the panel of 4 experts
- /deep-dive   — Ask one expert to go in depth on a specific topic
- /panel       — Simulate a full panel interview with all 4 experts asking questions

## The 4 expert agents
See agents/ folder for full definitions:
- agents/statistics-expert.md
- agents/actigraphy-digital-expert.md
- agents/bipolar-clinical-expert.md
- agents/project-leader-expert.md

## Context files
- context/position.md       — The job posting and project description
- context/research-group.md — The research group and supervisor background
- context/ingebjorg.md      — Ingebjørg's background and strengths

## Rules
- No emojis in any output
- Always be concrete and specific — no vague generalities
- When simulating interview questions, make them genuinely challenging
- After each answer session, suggest what to study further
