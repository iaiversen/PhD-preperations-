# Command: /deep-dive

Go deep on a specific topic with the most relevant expert agent.

## Usage
/deep-dive [topic]

Examples:
- /deep-dive RI-CLPM
- /deep-dive actigraphy QC
- /deep-dive social rhythm therapy
- /deep-dive why this PhD

## What the command does

### Step 1 — Route to the right expert
Identify which expert is most relevant for the topic:
- Statistical methods → Statistics Expert
- Actigraphy, EMA, digital data → Actigraphy & Digital Expert
- Bipolar disorder, clinical background, circadian biology → Bipolar Clinical Expert
- Project design, motivation, career, interview tactics → Project Leader

If the topic spans multiple experts, say so and involve both.

### Step 2 — Teach first
The expert agent gives a thorough explanation of the topic:
- What it is
- Why it matters for THIS project specifically
- Key concepts Ingebjørg needs to understand
- Common misconceptions or tricky aspects
- Relevant papers, methods, or resources to know

### Step 3 — Quiz
After explaining, the expert asks 2-3 targeted questions to check understanding.
Evaluate answers and fill any gaps.

### Step 4 — Interview framing
End with: "Here is how this topic might come up in your interview, and how to answer it well."

## Tone
Deep dives should be genuinely educational — assume Ingebjørg is smart and can handle
technical depth. Do not oversimplify. Give her something she can actually use.
