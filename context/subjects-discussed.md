## Subjects

### Clinical

#### Euthymic Intervals (Euthymia)
**Definition:** Periods in bipolar disorder where the person is in a stable, relatively normal mood state — neither manic/hypomanic nor depressed. Historically considered "well periods," but research shows they are often not symptom-free: subsyndromal symptoms, cognitive deficits, and functional impairment frequently persist.

**Relevance to project:** The core research question depends on measuring what happens between episodes. If circadian disruption (measured by actigraphy) exists during euthymia, it may be a trait marker reflecting underlying vulnerability rather than a state-dependent symptom. Within-person variability in rest-activity rhythms during euthymia may predict episode onset — this is exactly what the intensive longitudinal design and idiographic methods of the project are built to capture.

#### Heritability of Bipolar Disorder
**Definition:** Heritability is a population-level statistic describing what proportion of the variance in who develops BD is attributable to genetic differences between people. For BD, twin studies estimate this at 70-80%. This does NOT mean an individual with a family member who has BD has a 70-80% chance of getting it (actual first-degree relative risk is roughly 5-10%). It means that across the population, 70-80% of the variation in who gets BD vs. who does not is explained by genetic variation.

**How twin studies produce this:** MZ twins (100% shared DNA) show ~40-70% concordance for BD; DZ twins (~50% shared DNA) show ~5-10%. The large gap drives the high heritability estimate. ACE models decompose variance into additive genetic (A ~0.70-0.80), shared environment (C ~0), and non-shared environment (E ~0.20-0.30). MZ concordance below 100% means genes create vulnerability, not destiny — environmental factors, epigenetics, and gene-environment interactions matter.

**Relevance to project:** The high heritability means circadian rhythm disturbances in euthymic patients could be endophenotypes — heritable, quantifiable traits sitting between genotype and clinical phenotype. If actigraphic measures are disrupted in BD patients during euthymia AND in unaffected relatives, they may be intermediate phenotypes explaining how genetic risk becomes clinical illness. The 20-30% environmental variance is where circadian disruption, lifestyle factors, and the variables this project measures could be operating.
