# Research Group and Supervisor Background

## Research group
Name: Kliniske symptommekanismer (Clinical symptom mechanisms)
Leader: Professor Trine Vik Lagerberg
Affiliation: Oslo University Hospital (OUS) / University of Oslo (UiO)

## Focus areas (based on known profile)
- Mechanisms underlying psychiatric symptoms, especially in psychosis spectrum and bipolar disorder
- Dimensional and transdiagnostic approaches to mental illness
- Longitudinal and naturalistic study designs
- Bridging basic science and clinical research

## OUS Seksjon for klinisk psykoseforskning
- Clinical psychosis research section — sits at the intersection of clinical psychiatry and research
- Recruits patients from active clinical services
- Collaborative environment: clinicians, researchers, PhD students working alongside each other

## Bipolarteamet at Nydalen DPS
- The clinical outpatient team for bipolar disorder at Nydalen District Psychiatric Center
- Serves as the recruitment and follow-up base for the PhD project
- Being embedded here means navigating both research and clinical cultures

## What Ingebjørg should know about this environment
- OUS is Norway's largest hospital and a major research hospital — high academic standards
- Clinical research means working with real patients and clinical staff, not just data
- The supervisor (Lagerberg) has a strong methodological profile — expect rigorous discussions
- The group likely values both technical competence and genuine clinical motivation
- Norwegian academic research environment: collaborative, less hierarchical than many international settings
