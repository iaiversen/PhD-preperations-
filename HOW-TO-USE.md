# How to Use This Workspace — OUS Interview Prep

## What this workspace does

This workspace runs a 4-expert agent team to help you prepare for your PhD stipendiat
interview at OUS for "The night and day of bipolar disorder."

The four experts are:
1. Statistics Expert — intensive longitudinal methods, causality, R
2. Actigraphy & Digital Expert — wearables, EMA, data quality
3. Bipolar Clinical Expert — psychiatry, circadian biology, BD knowledge
4. Research Project Leader — PhD navigation, motivation, interview tactics

They stay in character and ask you real questions. They evaluate your answers honestly.

---

## Before your first session

Fill in this file: context/ingebjorg.md

The agents need to know your background to give you tailored feedback. Without this,
they will work with what they know about the position but cannot calibrate to your gaps.
Takes 10-15 minutes. Do it before anything else.

---

## The three main modes

### /quiz — Practice individual questions
Best for: targeted drilling, warming up, working on weak areas
How it works: agents take turns asking you one question at a time, evaluate your answer,
then move to the next. You stay in control of the pace.

### /panel — Full mock interview
Best for: when you feel ready to simulate the real thing
How it works: the Project Leader opens, then each expert takes a turn, then wrap-up with Q&A.
You get a full debrief at the end with your strongest and weakest moments.

### /deep-dive [topic] — Learn something specific
Best for: when you hit a gap and need to actually learn the content before practicing
How it works: the relevant expert teaches you the topic in depth, then tests you on it,
then frames how it will likely come up in the interview.

## Suggested prep schedule

### Week before interview (3-4 sessions recommended)

Session 1 — Orientation
- Fill in context/ingebjorg.md
- Run /prime
- Run /quiz in mixed mode — see where you stand across all domains

Session 2 — Plug the biggest gap
- Run /deep-dive on whichever topic you felt weakest in session 1
- Follow up with /quiz focused on that domain

Session 3 — Clinical and motivation
- Run /deep-dive on whatever clinical topic feels shakiest
- Run /deep-dive "why this PhD" — your narrative needs to be solid

Session 4 — Full dress rehearsal
- Run /panel from start to finish
- Review the debrief
- Note what to fix and practice those specific answers

Review your outputs/ files between sessions to reinforce what you learned.

---

## A note on using this well

The agents will be honest with you. If an answer is weak, they will say so.
That is the point. The interview panel will not be softer than these agents.

The goal is not to memorize answers — it is to genuinely understand the material
well enough that you can handle unexpected angles. The panel will go off-script.
So should you.
